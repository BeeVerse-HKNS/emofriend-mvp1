The Problem → Our Method

Three gaps. One closed loop.

For every painpoint, EmoPlay follows a simple, defensible method: Narrow the signal → Detect risk early → Solve inside the golden window.

1 · Invisible Emotions

Teachers & parents lack real-time, objective emotional signals. By the time a child is flagged, the crisis has already escalated.

NarrowDaily 60-sec check-in + 25 FACS games flag the first deviation.
Detect2,082-term NLP scores risk vs. age-normed baseline.
SolveChild vault + CASEL radar alerts parent/teacher early.

2 · SEL Shortage + Unsafe AI

One counsellor can't cover 500+ students. Generic AI chatbots have no CASEL framework, no escalation — the unsafe "tree hole".

NarrowGame-based CASEL 5×17 + LEGO® Serious Play® reach every child.
DetectSafe AI 'Bee' with hard guardrails flags risk, never encourages harm.
SolveTeacher heatmap + human escalation — always in the loop.

3 · Broken Family–School Loop

Parents see tantrums at home; teachers see withdrawal in class; clinicians see a crisis months later. Golden windows are missed.

NarrowOne child-owned vault shares 30-day curves + CASEL radar.
DetectHome & school read the same early signal.
SolveBoth act on one story, inside the GZ 'Screen-Alert-Intervene' loop.
