Guangzhou Industrialisation · 25% weight

A First-in-Nation Law Created the Market

Guangzhou's Regulations on the Promotion of Mental Health for Primary & Secondary Students took effect 1 Nov 2024 — China's first local mental-health law (7 chapters, 35 articles). It mandates what EmoCare delivers.

3,806 schools · 2.93M students
  • ≥1 mental-health class per 2 weeks per class
  • 24-hour city-wide student psychological hotline
  • Annual universal screening (P4+) + one-file-per-student archive
  • Schools may ban smartphones on campus
  • 2024 autumn: city-wide "All-staff Mentor System" (1 teacher ≈ 15 students)
  • "Screen → Alert → Intervene" closed loop
