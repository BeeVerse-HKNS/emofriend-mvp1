Privacy & Safety · Compliance

A safe "tree hole", by design

Generic companion AI has driven real harm (Character.AI & ChatGPT minor-death cases; China's Aug-2025 ban on companion-AI emotional dependency). EmoPlay is engineered the opposite way.

Privacy-by-design

On-device / edge inference — no raw face video or voice stored. Aligned with PIPL (China), COPPA & GDPR-K (minors): data minimisation, verifiable parental consent, right to erasure.

Safe AI 'Bee'

Hard guardrails: no medical advice, no encouragement of harm, no emotional dependency. A child is always told "a grown-up is here too."

Human-in-the-loop

Auto-flagged risk routes to a parent, teacher or the city hotline — never to an autonomous chatbot decision. The AI supports; it does not replace adults.

No dark patterns

No behavioural advertising, no manipulative loops, no third-party tracking. Engagement comes from play and growth, not compulsion.
